Our Story

ALI GEMS is a premier gems and jewellery export company based in the heart of Jaipur, Rajasthan - India's gemstone capital. Founded by Ali Hussain in 2023, we have quickly established ourselves as a trusted name in the international gemstone market.

Located at 1738 Near Aadamsha Hospital, Ghat Gate, Jaipur, our company specializes in sourcing and exporting premium quality gemstones to clients worldwide. With a projected annual turnover of 50-60 lakhs, we combine traditional expertise with modern business practices.

Our commitment to quality, authenticity, and customer satisfaction has made us a preferred choice for gemstone buyers around the world. We take pride in our GST registration (08BMSPH9984D1ZB) and our dedication to ethical business practices.
