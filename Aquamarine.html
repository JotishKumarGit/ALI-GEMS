Aquamarine

Aquamarine is a beautiful blue to blue-green gemstone from the beryl family, known for its clarity, calming color, and elegant appearance.

  • Stone Type: Aquamarine
  • Color: Light Blue to Blue-Green
  • Cut: Oval / Round / Emerald
  • Clarity: Eye Clean
  • Usage: Rings, Pendants, Bracelets

Aquamarine is believed to symbolize peace, courage, and clear communication. It is widely used in fine jewelry and is especially popular for its soothing tones.
